# Digital Product Marketing 101 — Condensed Cheat Sheet

> A 15-minute read that gives you the *basics* of marketing digital products,
> distilled from Russell Brunson's funnel playbooks, the Iman Gadzhi / Brunson
> teardowns, and the sell-before-you-build doctrine. Read this before the courses.

---

## 1. The one idea that organizes everything: the Value Ladder

> **Stack offers from a low-priced entry (the bait) up to a high-priced back-end.**
> Each rung naturally leads to the next. Your customer-acquisition cost is paid
> back across all rungs, not just the first one.

The ladder is the *framework*. Every funnel you build is one rung of it.

| Rung | Name | Price | Job |
|------|------|-------|-----|
| 0 | **Lead magnet** | Free | Capture the email. Build trust. |
| 1 | **Tripwire** | $1–$27 | Convert a cold visitor into a *first-time buyer*. Its job is NOT to make money — it's to turn a prospect into a customer, which **doubles/triples** conversion on the core offer. |
| 2 | **Core** | $97–$997 | The main product. The real business. |
| 3 | **Back-end** | $2,997+ | High-ticket. The destination. |

**The trap beginners fall into:** they build only the core ($99) and skip the
tripwire. The playbook says the tripwire is what makes the core convert.

---

## 2. The 8 Brunson funnel archetypes (the "how")

These are the reusable structures. You don't invent funnels — you pick an
archetype and fill in your offer.

1. **Tripwire funnel** — low-priced entry ($1–$27) promising one finished outcome. Converts cold traffic to first-time buyers.
2. **Challenge funnel** — free, time-boxed, live event with a clear milestone promise ("earn your first $1,000"). Builds urgency + community + FOMO. Sells the core at the end.
3. **Perfect Webinar funnel** — 60–90 min one-to-many sales presentation. Epiphany bridge → 3 secrets that break false beliefs → stacked offer close. Converts 2–10% of registrants on $497–$2,997 offers.
4. **Value Ladder funnel** — the meta-pattern that organizes all the others (see above).
5. **Free + shipping tripwire** — "free" product, pay shipping. Pulls in ideal prospects at near-zero risk.
6. **Software-as-finish-line** — every funnel points at your software/tool as the destination.
7. **Recurring-edition funnel** — run the same challenge/event on a cadence so it's evergreen, not one-off.
8. **Co-branded funnel** — pair with a complementary authority to expand reach.

## 3. The offer-first doctrine (what to build BEFORE the funnel)

> **"It's not the product that makes money. It's who you make it for and how you package it."**

1. **Narrow ICP** — one tight buyer (age band, country, occupation). Not "everyone."
2. **One-line pitch** — "X is your [category]." One sentence.
3. **One concrete number promise** — "$373/day", "earn your first $1,000", "protects a $200,000 decision." Specific beats vague.
4. **Low-price hook** — $1 for 7 days, or a cheap tripwire.
5. **Risk-reversal guarantee** — "If you don't get [outcome] in 7 days, we refund you." Removes the fear.
6. **Price on the size of the problem, not the effort** — solve a $100k problem, charge $20k; solve a $5 annoyance, charge $500 feels like a rip-off.

## 4. The two authority poles (how you earn trust)

From the Iman Gadzhi + Russell Brunson teardowns:

| Pole | Authority comes from | Example |
|------|---------------------|---------|
| **System / offer authority** | Books, software, proven frameworks | Russell Brunson (ClickFunnels, funnel playbooks) |
| **Story / personal-brand authority** | Raw origin story, income proof, vulnerability | Iman Gadzhi ("I started in 2015 before I'd found any success... I don't hide anything") |

**Lesson:** you don't need both. Pick the one that fits you. A raw honest story
builds trust faster than polish; a proven system builds trust faster than hype.

## 5. The funnel mechanics that actually convert (reusable)

1. **Free, low-friction entry** — a live challenge or free tool, not a paywall.
2. **One clear milestone promise** — not a feature list.
3. **Layered upsells** — free → low-ticket → high-ticket.
4. **Urgency & scarcity** — countdown timers, "first 777", live-only bonuses.
5. **Proof stacking** — real client stories with specific numbers.
6. **Demo-first content** — show the machine working live, don't teach theory.
7. **Community delivery** — WhatsApp/Discord group creates belonging + FOMO.
8. **Recurring editions** — the same funnel on a cadence = evergreen.

---

## 6. The distribution rule (when to push)

> **"Do not push any channel until the offer is buyable AND analytics is installed.**
> Traffic to a site with no checkout is a dead end."

Order of operations:
1. **Buyable offer** (real checkout, no waitlist)
2. **Analytics** (so you can measure which channel converts)
3. **Then distribute** (launch platforms → SEO → communities → directories → newsletters)

---

## 7. The sell-before-you-build loop

1. Define the offer first (ICP, pitch, hook, guarantee).
2. Ship a real, buyable thing fast — not polish.
3. Launch post: identity hook + personal story + proven format + image + URL in comments.
4. Direct outreach: two templates (business vs personal), sent individually.
5. Community post with a free-value hook.
6. 1–2 simple short-form videos (no paid ads).
7. Wait 48–72h. **The only signal that matters: someone put their card in.**
8. Decide kill/continue on **opportunity cost**, not ego.

## 10. The one-line summary

> **Build a value ladder (free → tripwire → core → back-end), lead with one
> concrete number promise, earn trust through story or system, make it buyable
> + measurable BEFORE you push traffic, and let the market signal — not polish —
> tell you whether to keep going.**
