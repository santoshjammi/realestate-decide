# Funnel Frameworks for a $29–$99 Digital Calculator Product

**Product:** RealEstateDecide.com — real-estate decision calculators for US investors.
**Value ladder:** Rung 0 free lead magnet (Rent vs Buy calc + 1% Rule cheat sheet, Brevo capture) → Rung 1 $29 tripwire (House-Flip calculator) → Rung 2 $99 core (suite of 4 calculators) → Rung 3 $497 back-end (done-with-you property analysis).
**Scope:** Four gurus — Hormozi (offer/value/pricing), Brunson (funnel archetypes), Sam O'Halloran (paid traffic for info products), plus pricing-psychology for the $99 tool. Each section gives (a) the framework, (b) mechanics/templates, (c) copy formulas, (d) mapping to this product.

---

## 1. Alex Hormozi — Offer, Value & Price Engineering

**Primary sources:** *$100M Offers* (Acquisition.com Publishing, July 2021); free Offers course at acquisition.com/training/offers; YAP Podcast interview (youngandprofiting.com).

### (a) Core framework: The Value Equation
Perceived value is a fraction — maximize the top, minimize the bottom:

```
                Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement
    Value  =  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                          Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice
```

### (b) Mechanics / templates
- **Front-load the dream outcome**, not features: "Know in 8 minutes whether a deal cash-flows" beats "features 4 calculators."
- **Specificity beats claims:** "Save 3 hours and avoid $12,000 in underwriting mistakes" beats "great calculator."
- **Value stack:** list each calculator + bonus with a real $ value, total them (~$297–$497 for a $99 suite), then price at $99. A clean 3–5× stack is credible for $99 (10× is too aggressive).
- **Guarantee stack layers:**
  - *Unconditional:* "If you don't love it, you don't pay. Your money back, no questions, no forms."
  - *ROI/outcome:* "If the tool doesn't save you [X dollars] on your next deal analysis, we'll refund the full purchase."
  - *Service-level:* "Every calculation method updated yearly so your numbers stay current — free for life of license."
  - `"That's $[total] in value. Today, just $[price]."`
  - Bonuses: "Free bonus (#$ value): [deliverable] — yours to keep even if you ask for a refund."

### (c) Copy formulas
- **Guarantee stack:** money-back + saves-a-bad-deal + yearly updates → converts the two buyer fears: "is it accurate?" and "will it help me?"
- **MAGIC name:** Result + timeframe + container, e.g. "The Flip Finder: know if a property profits in 8 minutes."
- **Anchor vs the cost of the problem:** "One underwriting mistake on a flip averages $8,000–$15,000. This $29 calculator is less than 1% of that."

### (d) Mapping to RealEstateDecide
- Numerator/denominator: calculator's denominator is the lever — instant results (Time Delay ≈ 0) + zero effort (type 3 numbers, get the answer). Lead with that.
- **Prove "likelihood"** with a deal before/after screenshot; show the flip-calc "estimated profit" output; a guarantee removes the residual doubt.
- **$99 suite stack:** 4 calculators + 1% Rule + comp-cheat-sheet + annual tax/rate update + private investor PDF export ≈ $297–$497 "value", priced $99.

## 2. Russell Brunson — Funnel Archetypes & the Value Ladder

**Primary sources:** *DotCom Secrets* (2015) and dotcomsecrets.com (live free+shipping funnel); *Expert Secrets* / Perfect Webinar; Funnel Scripts / ClickFunnels education.

### (a) Core framework: Value Ladder + 3 workhorse funnels
Ladder: Bait (free/low) → Front-end (cheap) → Middle → Back-end. Each rung is a *natural next yes*. His three business-critical funnels treat cold, warm, and hot audiences:
- **Tripwire funnel (cold):** free+shipping or ~$7 offer after a lead magnet. First payment is the psychological threshold — once someone pays, they're a customer and buy again.
- **Core/Value-ladder funnel (warm):** front-end → order bump → upsell → downsell → back-end.
- **Perfect Webinar (hot):** 60–90 min one-to-many; epiphany bridge, three secrets, stacked close; sweet spot $497–$2,997.

His **"23 funnel pieces"** are the Lego blocks: squeeze page, opt-in, free+shipping two-step, order bump, upsell, downsell, tripwire, Self-Liquidating Offer ($37–$97), webinar reg, free account, exit pop, etc.

### (b) Mechanics (real DotCom Secrets numbers — usable baseline)
From the dotcomsecrets.com free+shipping funnel:
1. **Free + shipping bait:** free hardcover book, buyer pays $9.95 shipping — ~14% purchase rate. (Even "free with shipping" qualifies buyers and lifts the next conversion.)
2. **Order bump:** +$37 audiobook on the order form — 28% of checkout visitors took it.
3. **Upsell:** ~$97 higher offer raised average cart. *Trade-off rule: sell higher price/lower conversion OR lower price/higher conversion.*
4. **Downsell:** a lower-ticket "no" alternative to recover upsell-refusers.
5. Each subsequent yes is more likely because the buyer paid once.

**Tripwire rule (DigitalMarketer/CartFlows):** lead magnet and tripwire must be *directly related* or conversion collapses. Typical upsell rates 5–15%+ warm.

### (c) Copy formulas
- **Perfect Webinar title = the offer at registration:** "How [specific audience] [specific outcome] in [specific time] using [specific mechanism]."
- **Webinar skeleton (timed):** min 0–5 restate the big promise → epiphany bridge → min 20–50 teach *three secrets*, each breaking a false belief → min 50–55 transition → min 55–75 stack the offer, deliverable-per-slide with anchored prices ("that's $9,997 in value"), then price as fraction ("today, $1,997").
- **Order-bump:** "Add [thing] for just $[X] — you're already here, and it saves you [time/money]."
- **Upsell:** "Do you want the [core] plus the [upsell] that solves [adjacent pain] for one small extra fee?"
- **Downsell:** "Not ready for the full [suite]? Get the [single calculator] for just $[tripwire price]."

### (d) Mapping to RealEstateDecide (your ladder IS a Brunson structure — add the missing mechanics)
- **Rung 0 → Rung 1:** free → $29 House-Flip tripwire. Rule: the cheat sheet *must* reference the flip calc so the next yes is obvious ("Here's the 1% Rule — now run your own flip in 3 minutes").
- **Order bump on $29 checkout:** add a 2nd calculator (Cash-on-Cash / BRRRR) for +$19. Modest, directly related, high take-rate at this price.
- **Upsell after $29:** full $99 suite discounted to $79 "one-time inside" (anchor vs $497 stack). Even 10–15% warm take makes the tripwire self-liquidating.
- **Downsell:** refuse $99 → offer individual $29 calculators so you keep *a* yes.
- **Back-end $497 = Perfect Webinar territory (not VSL):** three secrets map cleanly — Secret 1 breaks "you need a full team to underwrite" (vehicle), Secret 2 breaks "you need a bigger portfolio to justify analysis" (internal), Secret 3 breaks "tools can't replace an underwriter's judgment" (external). Offer done-with-you analysis as the stacked close.
- **Don't force a webinar on $29/$99** — under ~$497 a clean sales page/VSL out-converts a webinar.

## 3. Paid-Traffic Practitioner — Sam O'Halloran (info-product Meta/YouTube/TikTok ads)

**Sources:** Sam O'Halloran, "Scale With Paid Ads in 2025"/"Info Product Ad" training; his public scaling-playbook thread (rattibha.com thread 1544307111164874752). Working media buyer scaling info products to seven figures.

### (a) Core framework: one offer + paid cold traffic + creative iteration
Five scaling rules (paraphrased from his published thread):
1. **One funnel/focus for ≥6 months** — launching new offers weekly kills momentum.
2. **Paid traffic to cold audience is the only control lever** — organic leaves you at the mercy of the algorithm.
3. **Always create new hooks/angles** — a winning angle ages; test hooks hitting different segments.
4. **Split testing is a full-time job** — test hooks, subject lines, images, pricing, checkout — not just creative.
5. **Optimize the back-end (upsells, LTV)** so the front end can run at break-even.

### (b) Mechanics for low-ticket/info products (<$100)
- **3-part funnel** for high-competition niches: hook → retain/reward → CTA.
- **Short-form creative surviving the cold feed:** hook in the first 2–3 seconds (value equation in the first lines), then proof, then CTA.
- **Hybrid retargeting loop** (TikTok + Meta) to lower CAC on warmed users.
- **Scale winners incrementally**, never cliff-jump budgets.
- **Low-ticket economics:** sell $29/$99 at break-even or small loss front-end; profit lives in the $497 back-end and email list.

### (c) Copy formulas for a calculator ad
- **Hook (2–3 sec, on-screen + voice):** "You're about to buy a [property] and you're guessing the numbers." / "This free 8-minute check just saved me from a $40,000 mistake."
- **Body (retain):** show the calc in action — type purchase price, rehab, ARV → see "Estimated profit: +$XX,XXX" on screen. (Proof *is* the demonstration — the tool demos itself, no testimonials needed.)
- **CTA:** "Grab the deal analyzer that does it in 8 minutes — $29."
- **Message-match rule:** ad hook angle must match the landing-page headline exactly (the biggest cold-traffic killer when mismatched).

### (d) Mapping to RealEstateDecide
- **Run ONE funnel for a quarter:** lead magnet → $29 flip tripwire → bump → $99 upsell → $497 back-end. Don't rotate offers weekly.
- **Cold paid traffic to the free calculator** (not straight to the paywall): capture the email, warm the list with Brevo, then sell. A free-calc ad converts cheaper than a $29 direct-sale ad and starts the ladder.
- **Budget:** start small ($20–30/day), kill poor-ROAS angles fast, scale the strongest hook; let the $497 back-end and email upsells carry margin.
- **Proof mechanism:** the tool demoing itself in the ad beats static claims — show real numbers being typed.

## 4. Positioning & Premium Pricing for the $99 Tool

**Sources:** SaaS pricing psychology — anchoring, decoy/tiering (getmonetizely.com "SaaS Pricing Psychology"; payproglobal.com "Price Anchoring for SaaS"), trial/freemium/reverse-trial models (smollaunch.com; getlago.com).

### (a) Core framework: anchor + good-better-best + match model to churn
- **Anchoring:** the first number the buyer sees becomes the ref (research suggests the first anchor shifts willingness-to-pay ~30–50%). Lead with premium, then discount.
- **Good-Better-Best / Rule of Three:** premium anchor + middle target + basic entry set the frame. A premium anchor *not really purchasable* is dismissed — it must be real.
- **Decoy:** an inferior mid tier priced near the premium makes the target the obvious value pick.
- **Model choice:** freemium only with volume (you're already freemium at Rung 0); free trial suits recurring value (a static calculator isn't recurring); reverse trial suits product-led tools; **one-time suits "compute a number now"; subscription suits ongoing data/updates.**

### (b) Mechanics for premium-positioning a calculator
1. **Three real tiers / anchor:** show a premium "Pro/For Teams" tier above $99 so $99 reads cheap — e.g. $99 Core (4 calculators), $197 Pro (+ portfolio tracker + bulk analysis), $497 Done-With-You. The $497 makes $99 a no-brainer.
2. **Anchor vs the outcome:** reference "X,XXX you just saved on one bad deal," not another software price. "One avoided bad flip pays for this 40×."
3. **One-time for calculators; subscription/fee only for the back-end.** One-time $99 with lifetime access + yearly data updates (the "service-level" bonus). The $497 done-with-you is inherently service → paid engagement.
4. **Avoid under-$100 subscription churn:** a $99/yr subscription on a tool used a few times/year invites churn at renewal. One-time removes the churn question entirely.
5. **Charm pricing vs premium:** $99 (left-digit "under $100") is fine for impulse/low-ticket; don't overuse $9 endings if you want premium perception — reserve charm ending for the tripwire.

### (c) Copy formulas for the pricing page
- **Tier labels that sell the middle:** "Core [recommended] · Pro · Done-With-You."
- **ROI anchor line:** "Analyze 10 deals before your first purchase decision and this pays for itself 10×."
- **Stack-then-anchor:** "$497+ in value → Today $99, one-time." (List the 4 calculators + bonuses with prices first.)
- **Risk-reversal on one-time:** "Try every calculator for 30 days. If it doesn't pay for itself, we refund you."
- **Upgrade-path line:** "Not sure? Buy the $29 House-Flip calculator first — we'll credit it toward your $99 suite."

### (d) Recommended pricing shape for RealEstateDecide
- **Keep:** Rung 0 free (freemium/trial layer), Rung 1 $29 tripwire, Rung 2 $99 core, Rung 3 $497 back-end.
- **Add:** a visible $197 Pro tier *above* $99 (real, purchasable) to anchor the core as the value pick; and a $29-credit-toward-$99 mechanic so the tripwire de-risks the core.
- **Payment model: one-time for calculators** (lifetime license + yearly market-rate updates). Subscription only on the $497 done-with-you engagement.
- **Price the stack, not the features:** anchor against avoided-deal losses, then show $497 stack → $99. One clean ROI line beats a features table.

## 5. Consolidated Playbook (one-pager for implementation)

1. **$29 tripwire page:** Hook = "Know if a flip profits in 8 minutes." Value-equation hero: instant, zero-effort. Guarantee small (14-day, since it's $29). Order bump +$19 for a 2nd calculator. Upsell to $79 suite. Downsell to individual calculators.
2. **$99 core page:** Anchor $497 stack of 4 calculators + bonuses → $99. Guarantee stack (money-back + saves-a-bad-deal + yearly updates). Tier table: $29 / $99 (pick) / $197 Pro / link to $497.
3. **$497 back-end:** Perfect Webinar framework — 3 secrets about underwriting beliefs → teach → stack → close. Only rung warranting a webinar.
4. **Traffic:** one funnel, ≥1 quarter, cold paid to the free calculator; Brevo warms; demo-the-tool ad creatives; hybrid retargeting; let the back-end carry margin.
5. **Sequencing rule:** every rung is a natural next yes — the cheat sheet references the flip calc, the tripwire references the suite, the suite references the done-with-you analysis.
